Elias had tended the same jagged rock for forty years, his days measured by the rhythm of crashing waves and the shifting light of storms. It was during a calm afternoon that he spotted a rusted metal cap bobbing near the shore. Curiosity overriding caution, he hauled it into his weathered workshop, only to find a heavy glass bottle inside.

With trembling hands, he twisted off the cork. A single sheet of paper fluttered out, written in elegant, looping script: *"To Elias, who keeps the light awake when the world sleeps. The storm you fear has passed. You are not alone."*

Elias stared at the words, his heart pounding against his ribs. For decades, he had felt isolated, a solitary sentinel against the vast ocean. But the message hummed with warmth, a strange sense of connection cutting through the years of solitude. He looked out at the sea, no longer seeing just dangerous depths, but a world waiting for someone to understand that he was never truly alone after all.
