To find the number of positive integers smaller than 1000 that are divisible by 7 but not divisible by 11, we can follow these steps:

1.  **Define the range**: We are looking for integers $n$ such that $1 \le n < 1000$. This is equivalent to finding integers in the set $\{1, 2, ..., 999\}$.

2.  **Count numbers divisible by 7**:
    The number of positive integers less than or equal to $N$ that are divisible by $k$ is given by $\lfloor \frac{N}{k} \rfloor$.
    Here, $N = 999$ and $k = 7$.
    $$ \text{Count}_7 = \left\lfloor \frac{999}{7} \right\rfloor $$
    Performing the division: $999 \div 7 = 142.71...$
    So, $\text{Count}_7 = 142$.

3.  **Count numbers divisible by both 7 and 11**:
    A number is divisible by both 7 and 11 if and only if it is divisible by their least common multiple (LCM). Since 7 and 11 are prime, $\text{LCM}(7, 11) = 7 \times 11 = 77$.
    We need to count integers less than or equal to 999 that are divisible by 77.
    $$ \text{Count}_{77} = \left\lfloor \frac{999}{77} \right\rfloor $$
    Performing the division: $999 \div 77 \approx 12.97...$
    So, $\text{Count}_{77} = 12$.

4.  **Apply the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion**:
    The set of numbers divisible by 7 but *not* by 11 is the set of numbers divisible by 7 minus the set of numbers divisible by both 7 and 11.
    $$ \text{Result} = \text{Count}_7 - \text{Count}_{77} $$
    $$ \text{Result} = 142 - 12 = 130 $$

The number of such integers is 130.

ANSWER: 130
