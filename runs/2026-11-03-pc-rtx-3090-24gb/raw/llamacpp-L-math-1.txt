To find the average speed over the whole trip, we need to calculate the total distance traveled and divide it by the total time taken.

**Step 1: Calculate the total distance.**
The train travels two segments:
1.  First segment: 180 km
2.  Second segment: 120 km

Total Distance = $180 \text{ km} + 120 \text{ km} = 300 \text{ km}$

**Step 2: Calculate the total time.**
The train takes two durations:
1.  First segment: 2.5 hours
2.  Second segment: 1.5 hours

Total Time = $2.5 \text{ hours} + 1.5 \text{ hours} = 4.0 \text{ hours}$

**Step 3: Calculate the average speed.**
The formula for average speed is:
$$ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} $$

Substituting the values:
$$ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{300 \text{ km}}{4.0 \text{ hours}} $$
$$ \text{Average Speed} = 75 \text{ km/h} $$

ANSWER: 75
