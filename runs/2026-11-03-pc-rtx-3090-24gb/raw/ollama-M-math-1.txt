To find the average speed of the train over the whole trip, we use the formula:

$$
\text{Average speed} = \frac{\text{Total distance}}{\text{Total time}}
$$

### Step 1: Calculate total distance
$$
180 \, \text{km} + 120 \, \text{km} = 300 \, \text{km}
$$

### Step 2: Calculate total time
$$
2.5 \, \text{hours} + 1.5 \, \text{hours} = 4 \, \text{hours}
$$

### Step 3: Calculate average speed
$$
\text{Average speed} = \frac{300 \, \text{km}}{4 \, \text{hours}} = 75 \, \text{km/h}
$$

ANSWER: 75
