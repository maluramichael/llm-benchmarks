Marked price = Original Cost + Markup Amount

**Step 2: Calculate the final discounted price.**

The discount is 10% off the marked price.
Discount amount = $70 \times 0.10 = $7$.
Final price = Marked Price - Discount Amount
Final price = $70 - $7 = $63$.
