To find the final price of the jacket, we need to follow the sequence of price changes step-by-step.

**Step 1: Calculate the marked-up price.**
The original cost of the jacket is $50.
The shop marks it up by 40%.
Markup amount = $50 \times 0.40 = $20$.
Marked price = Original Cost + Markup Amount
Marked price = $50 + $20 = $70$.

**Step 2: Calculate the final discounted price.**
The shop discounts the marked price by 10%.
Discount amount = Marked Price $\times 0.10$
Discount amount = $70 \times 0.10 = $7$.
Final Price = Marked Price - Discount Amount
Final Price = $70 - $7 = $63$.

Alternatively, you can calculate the final price directly using multipliers:
Final Price = $50 \times (1 + 0.40) \times (1 - 0.10)$
Final Price = $50 \times 1.40 \times 0.90$
Final Price = $70 \times 0.90$
Final Price = $63$
