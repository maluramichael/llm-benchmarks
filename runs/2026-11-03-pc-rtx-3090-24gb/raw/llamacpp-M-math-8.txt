To find the number of **distinct arrangements** of the letters in the word **BANANA**, we start by noting that:

- The word **BANANA** has **6 letters**.
- The letters are: **B, A, N, A, N, A**.
- The letter **A** appears **3 times**, the letter **N** appears **2 times**, and the letter **B** appears **1 time**.

The total number of **distinct arrangements** of these letters is given by the **multinomial coefficient**:

$$
\frac{6!}{3! \cdot 2! \cdot 1!}
$$

Now compute:

- $6! = 720$
- $3! = 6$
- $2! = 2$
- $1! = 1$

$$
\frac{720}{6 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{720}{12} = 60
$$

**ANSWER: 60**
