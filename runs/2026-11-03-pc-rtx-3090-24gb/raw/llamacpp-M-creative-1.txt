Every night, old Captain Elias tended the lighthouse on the edge of a stormy coast. One foggy evening, as he swept the rocks below, his boot caught on something smooth. He dug and unearthed a bottle, its cork sealed with a rusted wire. With trembling hands, he pried it open.

Inside was a letter, yellowed with age. The handwriting was neat and urgent: *“If you find this, I’m lost. My name is Clara. I sailed from Norway, but the storm took my ship. I’m on a nearby island. Please, help me.”*

Elias’s heart pounded. He hurried to the radio room and sent a distress call. Days later, a rescue team found Clara, stranded but alive, on a desolate island.

When she arrived at the lighthouse, she clutched the bottle tightly. “You saved my life,” she whispered. Elias simply smiled. The sea had brought them together, and the message in a bottle had been the thread that tied their fates.
