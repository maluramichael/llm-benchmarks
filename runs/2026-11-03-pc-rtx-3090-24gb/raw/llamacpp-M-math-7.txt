Let the width of the rectangle be $ w $. Then the length is $ 2w $.

The perimeter of a rectangle is given by:

$$
P = 2(\text{length} + \text{width}) = 2(2w + w) = 2(3w) = 6w
$$

We are told the perimeter is 54:

$$
6w = 54
$$

Solving for $ w $:

$$
w = \frac{54}{6} = 9
$$

Now, the length is $ 2w = 2 \times 9 = 18 $.

The area of the rectangle is:

$$
\text{Area} = \text{length} \times \text{width} = 18 \times 9 = 162
$$

ANSWER: 162
