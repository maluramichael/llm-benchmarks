To find the sum of all integers from 1 to 100 that are divisible by 3 or by 5, we can use the Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion.

Let $S_3$ be the set of integers from 1 to 100 divisible by 3.
Let $S_5$ be the set of integers from 1 to 100 divisible by 5.
We want to find the sum of the elements in the union $S_3 \cup S_5$.

The formula for the sum of the union is:
$$ \text{Sum}(S_3 \cup S_5) = \text{Sum}(S_3) + \text{Sum}(S_5) - \text{Sum}(S_3 \cap S_5) $$

Note that $S_3 \cap S_5$ is the set of integers divisible by both 3 and 5, which means they are divisible by $\text{lcm}(3, 5) = 15$. Let's call this set $S_{15}$.

So, the formula becomes:
$$ \text{Sum}(S_3 \cup S_5) = \text{Sum}(S_3) + \text{Sum}(S_5) - \text{Sum}(S_{15}) $$

**Step 1: Calculate $\text{Sum}(S_3)$**
The multiples of 3 from 1 to 100 are $3, 6, 9, \dots, 99$.
This is an arithmetic progression with first term $a_1 = 3$, common difference $d = 3$, and last term $a_n = 99$.
To find the number of terms $n$:
$$ a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d $$
$$ 99 = 3 + (n-1)3 $$
$$ 96 = (n-1)3 $$
$$ 32 = n-1 \implies n = 33 $$
The sum of an arithmetic series is given by $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a_1 + a_n)$.
$$ \text{Sum}(S_3) = \frac{33}{2}(3 + 99) = \frac{33}{2}(102) = 33 \times 51 $$
$$ 33 \times 51 = 33(50 + 1) = 1650 + 33 = 1683 $$

**Step 2: Calculate $\text{Sum}(S_5)$**
The multiples of 5 from 1 to 100 are $5, 10, 15, \dots, 100$.
This is an arithmetic progression with first term $a_1 = 5$, common difference $d = 5$, and last term $a_n = 100$.
To find the number of terms $n$:
$$ 100 = 5 + (n-1)5 $$
$$ 95 = (n-1)5 $$
$$ 19 = n-1 \implies n = 20 $$
$$ \text{Sum}(S_5) = \frac{20}{2}(5 + 100) = 10(105) = 1050 $$

**Step 3: Calculate $\text{Sum}(S_{15})$**
The multiples of 15 from 1 to 100 are $15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90$.
This is an arithmetic progression with first term $a_1 = 15$, common difference $d = 15$, and last term $a_n = 90$.
To find the number of terms $n$:
$$ 90 = 15 + (n-1)15 $$
$$ 75 = (n-1)15 $$
$$ 5 = n-1 \implies n = 6 $$
$$ \text{Sum}(S_{15}) = \frac{6}{2}(15 + 90) = 3(105) = 315 $$

**Step 4: Combine the results**
$$ \text{Sum}(S_3 \cup S_5) = 1683 + 1050 - 315 $$
$$ 1683 + 1050 = 2733 $$
$$ 2733 - 315 = 2
