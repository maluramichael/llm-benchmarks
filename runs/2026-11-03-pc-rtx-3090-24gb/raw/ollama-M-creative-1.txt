Every night, old Captain Elias tended the lighthouse on the rugged coast of Blackmere. One stormy evening, as waves crashed against the rocks, he noticed something unusual glinting in the surf. With trembling hands, he pulled a bottle from the water, its cork still intact.

Inside was a yellowed note, written in elegant script: *“If you find this, please send help. I am stranded on the island of Driftmoor. My name is Clara. I’ve been here for weeks.”* The date was from three years ago.

Elias’s heart sank. He had heard rumors of a ghostly island, long abandoned. But as he read the message again, something stirred within him. The next morning, he set out in his boat, determined to find Clara. 

After hours of navigating through fog, he spotted a small island, and there, huddled under a tree, was a woman, alone and weak. Elias had found more than a message—he had found hope.
