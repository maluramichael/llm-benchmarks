We are asked to find the remainder when $ 3^{101} $ is divided by 7, i.e., compute $ 3^{101} \mod 7 $.

---

### Step 1: Use modular arithmetic to simplify the exponent

We can use **Fermat's Little Theorem**, which states that if $ p $ is a prime and $ a $ is not divisible by $ p $, then:

$$
a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \mod p
$$

Here, $ p = 7 $, and $ a = 3 $, which is not divisible by 7. So:

$$
3^6 \equiv 1 \mod 7
$$

---

### Step 2: Reduce the exponent modulo 6

Since $ 3^6 \equiv 1 \mod 7 $, we can write:

$$
3^{101} = 3^{6 \cdot 16 + 5} = (3^6)^{16} \cdot 3^5
$$

Now, modulo 7:

$$
(3^6)^{16} \equiv 1^{16} \equiv 1 \mod 7
$$

So:

$$
3^{101} \equiv 1 \cdot 3^5 \mod 7
$$

---

### Step 3: Compute $ 3^5 \mod 7 $

$$
3^1 = 3 \mod 7 \\
3^2 = 9 \equiv 2 \mod 7 \\
3^3 = 3 \cdot 2 = 6 \mod 7 \\
3^4 = 3 \cdot 6 = 18 \equiv 4 \mod 7 \\
3^5 = 3 \cdot 4 = 12 \equiv 5 \mod 7
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
3^{101} \equiv 5 \mod 7
$$

ANSWER: 5
