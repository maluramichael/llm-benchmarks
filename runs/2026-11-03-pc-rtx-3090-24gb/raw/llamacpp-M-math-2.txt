To find the sum of all integers from 1 to 100 that are divisible by **3 or 5**, we can use the **principle of inclusion and exclusion**.

---

### Step 1: Sum of numbers divisible by 3

We find the sum of all numbers from 1 to 100 that are divisible by 3.

- The numbers divisible by 3 from 1 to 100 are:  
  $ 3, 6, 9, \dots, 99 $

- This is an arithmetic sequence with:
  - First term $ a = 3 $
  - Common difference $ d = 3 $
  - Last term $ l = 99 $

- Number of terms $ n $:  
  $$
  n = \frac{99 - 3}{3} + 1 = \frac{96}{3} + 1 = 32 + 1 = 33
  $$

- Sum of the sequence:
  $$
  S_3 = \frac{n}{2} \cdot (a + l) = \frac{33}{2} \cdot (3 + 99) = \frac{33}{2} \cdot 102 = 33 \cdot 51 = 1683
  $$

---

### Step 2: Sum of numbers divisible by 5

- Numbers divisible by 5 from 1 to 100:  
  $ 5, 10, 15, \dots, 100 $

- Arithmetic sequence with:
  - $ a = 5 $
  - $ d = 5 $
  - $ l = 100 $

- Number of terms $ n $:  
  $$
  n = \frac{100 - 5}{5} + 1 = \frac{95}{5} + 1 = 19 + 1 = 20
  $$

- Sum of the sequence:
  $$
  S_5 = \frac{20}{2} \cdot (5 + 100) = 10 \cdot 105 = 1050
  $$

---

### Step 3: Sum of numbers divisible by both 3 and 5 (i.e., divisible by 15)

- Numbers divisible by 15 from 1 to 100:  
  $ 15, 30, 45, \dots, 90 $

- Arithmetic sequence with:
  - $ a = 15 $
  - $ d = 15 $
  - $ l = 90 $

- Number of terms $ n $:  
  $$
  n = \frac{90 - 15}{15} + 1 = \frac{75}{15} + 1 = 5 + 1 = 6
  $$

- Sum of the sequence:
  $$
  S_{15} = \frac{6}{2} \cdot (15 + 90) = 3 \cdot 105 = 315
  $$

---

### Step 4: Apply inclusion-exclusion principle

$$
\text{Total sum} = S_3 + S_5 - S_{15} = 1683 + 1050 - 315 = 2418
$$

---

**ANSWER: 2418**
