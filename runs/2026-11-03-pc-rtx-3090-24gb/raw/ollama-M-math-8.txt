The word **BANANA** has 6 letters, with the following letter frequencies:

- **B**: 1
- **A**: 3
- **N**: 2

The total number of **distinct arrangements** of the letters is given by the formula for permutations of a multiset:

$$
\frac{6!}{3! \cdot 2! \cdot 1!} = \frac{720}{6 \cdot 2 \cdot 1} = \frac{720}{12} = 60
$$

ANSWER: 60
