Let the two numbers be $x$ and $y$.

We are given two equations:
1) $x + y = 58$
2) $x - y = 14$

To find the values of $x$ and $y$, we can add the two equations together:
$(x + y) + (x - y) = 58 + 14$
$2x = 72$
$x = 36$

Now, substitute the value of $x$ back into the first equation to find $y$:
$36 + y = 58$
$y = 58 - 36$
$y = 22$

So, the two numbers are 36 and 22.

The question asks for their product:
Product $= x \times y$
Product $= 36 \times 22$

Calculation:
$36 \times 20 = 720$
$36 \times 2 = 72$
$720 + 72 = 792$

Alternatively:
$36 \times 22 = 36 \times (20 + 2) = 720 + 72 = 792$

ANSWER: 792
