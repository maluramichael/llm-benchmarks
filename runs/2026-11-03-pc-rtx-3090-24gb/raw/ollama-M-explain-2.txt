- The next time you call `fib(5)`, it doesn't recalculate it — it just returns the stored value.
- This is especially helpful for recursive functions like `fib`, where the same values are computed many times.

### 🐍 **Why it's faster with the cache:**

#### ✅ **With the cache (`@lru_cache`):**
