Let $L$ be the length and $W$ be the width of the rectangle.

We are given two pieces of information:
1. The perimeter is 54. The formula for the perimeter of a rectangle is $P = 2(L + W)$.
   So, $2(L + W) = 54$.
   Dividing both sides by 2, we get:
   $L + W = 27$

2. The length is twice the width.
   So, $L = 2W$

Now, substitute the second equation into the first equation:
$2W + W = 27$
$3W = 27$
$W = 9$

Now find the length $L$:
$L = 2W = 2(9) = 18$

The area $A$ of the rectangle is given by $A = L \times W$:
$A = 18 \times 9$
$A = 162$

ANSWER: 162
