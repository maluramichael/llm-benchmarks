To find the average speed over the whole trip, we use the formula:

$$ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} $$

**Step 1: Calculate the total distance.**
The train travels 180 km and then 120 km.
$$ \text{Total Distance} = 180 \text{ km} + 120 \text{ km} = 300 \text{ km} $$

**Step 2: Calculate the total time.**
The train travels for 2.5 hours and then 1.5 hours.
$$ \text{Total Time} = 2.5 \text{ hours} + 1.5 \text{ hours} = 4.0 \text{ hours} $$

**Step 3: Calculate the average speed.**
$$ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{300 \text{ km}}{4.0 \text{ hours}} = 75 \text{ km/h} $$

ANSWER: 75
