Let the two numbers be $x$ and $y$.
We are given the following system of equations:
1. $x + y = 58$
2. $x - y = 14$

To find the product $x \cdot y$, we can first solve for $x$ and $y$.
Adding the two equations together:
$(x + y) + (x - y) = 58 + 14$
$2x = 72$
$x = 36$

Substituting $x = 36$ into the first equation:
$36 + y = 58$
$y = 58 - 36$
$y = 22$

Now, calculate the product of the two numbers:
Product $= x \cdot y = 36 \cdot 22$

Calculation:
$36 \cdot 20 = 720$
$36 \cdot 2 = 72$
$720 + 72 = 792$

ANSWER: 792
