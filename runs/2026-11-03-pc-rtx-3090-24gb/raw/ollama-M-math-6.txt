We are asked to find the remainder when $ 3^{101} $ is divided by 7, i.e., compute $ 3^{101} \mod 7 $.

---

### Step 1: Use modular arithmetic to simplify powers of 3 modulo 7

We compute the powers of 3 modulo 7:

- $ 3^1 \mod 7 = 3 $
- $ 3^2 \mod 7 = 9 \mod 7 = 2 $
- $ 3^3 \mod 7 = (3^2 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = (2 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = 6 $
- $ 3^4 \mod 7 = (3^3 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = (6 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = 18 \mod 7 = 4 $
- $ 3^5 \mod 7 = (3^4 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = (4 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = 12 \mod 7 = 5 $
- $ 3^6 \mod 7 = (3^5 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = (5 \cdot 3) \mod 7 = 15 \mod 7 = 1 $

We see that:

$$
3^6 \equiv 1 \mod 7
$$

This means that the powers of 3 modulo 7 repeat every 6 steps. So:

$$
3^{6k} \equiv 1 \mod 7
$$

---

### Step 2: Use the repeating pattern to simplify $ 3^{101} \mod 7 $

We divide 101 by 6:

$$
101 \div 6 = 16 \text{ remainder } 5
$$

So:

$$
3^{101} = 3^{6 \cdot 16 + 5} = (3^6)^{16} \cdot 3^5
$$

Using $ 3^6 \equiv 1 \mod 7 $, we get:

$$
(3^6)^{16} \equiv 1^{16} \equiv 1 \mod 7
$$

So:

$$
3^{101} \equiv 1 \cdot 3^5 \mod 7
$$

Earlier, we found:

$$
3^5 \mod 7 = 5
$$

---

### Final Answer:

$$
3^{101} \mod 7 = 5
$$

ANSWER: 5
