Let the two numbers be $ x $ and $ y $, where $ x > y $.

We are given:
1. $ x + y = 58 $
2. $ x - y = 14 $

Add the two equations:
$$
(x + y) + (x - y) = 58 + 14
$$
$$
2x = 72 \Rightarrow x = 36
$$

Substitute $ x = 36 $ into the first equation:
$$
36 + y = 58 \Rightarrow y = 22
$$

Now, find the product:
$$
x \cdot y = 36 \cdot 22 = 792
$$

ANSWER: 792
