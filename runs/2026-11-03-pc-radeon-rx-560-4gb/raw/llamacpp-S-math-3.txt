To find the final price of the jacket, we will follow these steps:

1.  **Calculate the marked price:**
    The shop buys the jacket for \$50 and marks it up by 40%.
    Markup amount = $50 \times 0.40 = \$20$.
    Marked price = Cost + Markup = $50 + 20 = \$70$.

2.  **Calculate the final price after the discount:**
    The shop discounts the marked price by 10%.
    Discount amount = $70 \times 0.10 = \$7$.
    Final price = Marked price - Discount = $70 - 7 = \$63$.

ANSWER: 63
