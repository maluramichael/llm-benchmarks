Elias had tended the same jagged rock for forty years, his days measured by the rhythm of the tide. One stormy evening, while cleaning the lantern room's glass, he discovered a small, weathered bottle wedged beneath the sand. Curiosity overriding his caution, he opened it to find a crumpled note and a single, perfect blue feather.

The message read: *"You are the only one who can hear the song beneath the waves. The light is not just for ships; it is a key."* Elias felt a strange vibration in the floorboards, syncing with the distant ocean hum. He realized the lighthouse wasn't merely guiding vessels; it was amplifying a hidden frequency. With a flick of his switch, the beam didn't just sweep the horizon; it revealed swirling currents of light in the dark water, turning the lonely keeper into a conductor of an unseen symphony.
