Let the two numbers be $x$ and $y$.
We are given the following system of equations:
1. $x + y = 58$
2. $x - y = 14$

To find the product $x \cdot y$, we first need to determine the values of $x$ and $y$. We can solve this system by adding the two equations together:
$(x + y) + (x - y) = 58 + 14$
$2x = 72$
$x = 36$

Now, substitute the value of $x$ back into the first equation to find $y$:
$36 + y = 58$
$y = 58 - 36$
$y = 22$

The two numbers are 36 and 22. Now, we calculate their product:
$36 \times 22 = 792$

ANSWER: 792
