### llama.cpp 0.7.0-dev (build 11909), Flint 40B-A2.5B UD-Q3_K_XL (F)

- **Model (exact tag):** unsloth/flint-GGUF, file flint-UD-Q3_K_XL.gguf (18.5 GB)
- **Quantization:** UD-Q3_K_XL (Unsloth Dynamic, 3 bit)
- **Class:** F (Flint, 40B MoE, about 2.5B active per token)
- **Max context:** 65,536 (completely on the GPU), 131,072 not safe by estimate (23,017 MB needed including 1.5 GB reserve, 22,490 MB free)
- **Cold load (s):** 7.35
- **TTFT cold (s):** 7.77
- **TTFT warm (s):** 2.58
- **TTFT with cache (s):** 0.19
- **Prompt processing tok/s:** 3,286
- **Generation tok/s:** 119.2
- **Generation tok/s at max context:** 96.1 (prompt processing 2,525)
- **Notes:** The file from the video "Can Flint Beat Qwen 3.8 27B?" (Weights & Wonders), according to the video the 4-bit file does not fit into 24 GB. llama-server with `-ngl 99 -fa on --fit off -np 1`, base context 16k, median of 3 runs, uses 19.9 GB VRAM. In the prompt cache test, 8,196 tokens came from the cache. llama-bench (`-p 512,8192 -n 256`, 3 repetitions): prompt processing 3,809 tok/s at 512 and 3,537 tok/s at 8,192 tokens, generation 128.2 tok/s, at a depth of 64,512 tokens prompt processing 2,042 and generation 97.7 tok/s.

The RTX 3090 has no FP4 units, yet generation is on par with llama.cpp.

## Summary

Flint generates about 120 tok/s on the RTX 3090, whether as UD-Q3_K_XL or Q4_K_M with llama.cpp or as NVFP4 with Ollama. That is almost three times Qwen3.8 27B with llama.cpp without MTP (42.2 tok/s) and almost twice Qwen3.8 27B with Ollama (66.4 tok/s). Prompt processing is also about twice as fast at about 3,300 tok/s. For 24 GB the 3-bit file from Unsloth is the best choice: as fast as Q4_K_M, but with room for 64k context (still 96 tok/s there), while Q4_K_M and NVFP4 fill the VRAM at 16k already. Only speed was measured, not the quality of the answers.
