Let $L$ be the length and $W$ be the width of the rectangle.

We are given two pieces of information:
1. The perimeter is 54. The formula for the perimeter of a rectangle is $P = 2(L + W)$.
   $$2(L + W) = 54$$
   Dividing both sides by 2:
   $$L + W = 27$$

2. The length is twice the width.
   $$L = 2W$$

Now, substitute the expression for $L$ from the second equation into the first equation:
$$(2W) + W = 27$$
$$3W = 27$$
$$W = 9$$

Now that we have the width, we can find the length:
$$L = 2 \times 9 = 18$$

To find the area ($A$) of the rectangle, we multiply the length by the width:
$$A = L \times W$$
$$A = 18 \times 9$$
$$A = 162$$

ANSWER: 162
