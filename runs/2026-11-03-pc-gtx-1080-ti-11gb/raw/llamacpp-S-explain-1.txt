This code implements the **Bubble Sort** algorithm.

### What it does in plain English:
It goes through the list multiple times, looking at adjacent pairs of numbers. If it finds a pair where the first number is larger than the second, it swaps them. This process repeats until the entire list is sorted in ascending order (smallest to largest).

Think of it like this: Larger numbers "bubble up" to the end of the list, while smaller numbers sink to the front.

### What it returns for the input `[3, 1, 2]`:
1.  **First Pass:**
    *   Compare 3 and 1: $3 > 1$, so they swap. List becomes `[1, 3, 2]`.
    *   Compare 3 and 2: $3 > 2$, so they swap. List becomes `[1, 2, 3]`.
2.  **Second Pass:**
    *   Compare 1 and 2: $1 < 2$, no swap.
    *   Compare 2 and 3: $2 < 3$, no swap.
3.  The list is now sorted.

**Return value:** `[1, 2, 3]`
