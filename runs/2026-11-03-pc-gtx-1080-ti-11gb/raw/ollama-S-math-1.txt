To find the average speed over the whole trip, we use the formula:

$$ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} $$

**Step 1: Calculate the total distance.**
The train travels two segments:
- First segment: $180 \text{ km}$
- Second segment: $120 \text{ km}$

$$ \text{Total Distance} = 180 + 120 = 300 \text{ km} $$

**Step 2: Calculate the total time.**
The train travels for two durations:
- First segment: $2.5 \text{ hours}$
- Second segment: $1.5 \text{ hours}$

$$ \text{Total Time} = 2.5 + 1.5 = 4.0 \text{ hours} $$

**Step 3: Calculate the average speed.**
Divide the total distance by the total time:

$$ \text{Average Speed} = \frac{300}{4} = 75 \text{ km/h} $$

ANSWER: 75
